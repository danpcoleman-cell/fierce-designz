The founder

Travi Morgan

A Los Angeles model, actor, and singer, and the face of Fierce Designz.

Fierce Designz is her clothing house: bold animal print, leather couture, Victorian volume, cat-ear hoodies, and space-age chrome, made for people who show up as themselves. Big personality. No apologies.

Her autobiography, Grind to Glory: My Story, is available as an eBook for $45.
